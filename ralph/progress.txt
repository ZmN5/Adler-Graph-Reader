## Codebase Patterns

- EPUB reader uses `react-reader` with epubjs
- PDF reader uses `pdfjs-dist` with virtual scrolling
- Three-column layout uses flexbox with Tailwind CSS
- Panel widths: Left 40%, Center 50%, Right 280px fixed
- State management in App.tsx, passed down to components

---

## 2025-03-31 - US-003 - Add collapsible reader panel

**What was implemented:**
- Added collapse/expand functionality to the left reader panel
- Collapse button (ChevronLeft) positioned at top-right of reader panel header
- Expanded button (ChevronRight) with book title in vertical text when collapsed
- Left panel collapses to 48px narrow bar
- Center panel expands automatically when left panel is collapsed
- Smooth 300ms CSS transition for collapse/expand animation
- Added new props to ThreeColumnLayout: `isLeftPanelCollapsed`, `onLeftPanelCollapseChange`, `leftPanelTitle`

**Files changed:**
- frontend/src/components/ThreeColumnLayout.tsx
- frontend/src/App.tsx

**Learnings for future iterations:**
- ThreeColumnLayout now supports collapsible left panel via props
- Flex classes change dynamically based on both left and right panel visibility
- When left is collapsed and right is shown: center panel gets flex-[9]
- When left is collapsed and right is hidden: center panel gets flex-[12]
- Use `writingMode: 'vertical-rl'` for vertical text in collapsed panel
- Absolute positioning for collapse button ensures it doesn't interfere with reader content
