## Codebase Patterns
- Chunk size constants are defined directly in pdf_parser.rs and epub_parser.rs (not centralized)

- Overlap ratio: 10% of chunk size (400 char overlap for 4000 char chunks)
- No sampling logic exists - all content is already processed
- LLM prompts include JSON Schema for structured output validation
- Validation logic filters out invalid concepts/relations with detailed logging
